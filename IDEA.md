# Lake Day

The goal is to build an app to optimize lake days to go out.
This should be an iOS app, but the stack that you choose is not limited.

## Pillars 
### Visibility
The goal with visibility is to allow the user to look at, and see near by lakes that are good candidates.
This will likely be used during the summer or in any warm places to ensure patron safety.

### Metrics
Key things it should show are:
1) Potential traffic at the lake
2) Lake safety (any water contamination, the water safety, and potential for bacterial water)
3) Water temperature
4) Rating for whether it is an ideal lake spot
5) Any dangers the lake is known for
6) Typical activities done on the lake (surfing, swimming, just beach, etc.)

### Other
Any other metrics that come to your mind, include it.
